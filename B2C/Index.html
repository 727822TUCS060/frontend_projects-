Frequently Asked Questions

Clear answers to common travel questions

What payment methods do you accept?

We accept all major credit/debit cards (Visa, Mastercard, Amex), bank transfers, Google Pay, PhonePe, and other digital wallets. We also offer EMI options for packages above ₹1 lakh.

Can I modify or cancel my booking?

Yes! Modifications are free up to 30 days before travel. Cancellations are accepted with our money-back guarantee up to 15 days before departure (minus processing fees).

Do you provide visa assistance?

Absolutely! We provide complete visa guidance and processing support for Thailand, Singapore, Malaysia, UAE, USA, and other countries. Our experts will guide you through every step.

What if my flight gets cancelled?

Our team provides 24/7 emergency support. We arrange rebooking at no extra cost and handle accommodation for unexpected delays. Your satisfaction is our priority.

Are meals included in packages?

Most packages include breakfast and some meals. Specific inclusions vary by package. You can customize meal preferences during booking. We cater to vegetarian, vegan, and dietary needs.

Do you offer travel insurance?

Yes! We offer comprehensive travel insurance covering medical emergencies, trip cancellations, and lost baggage. It's optional but highly recommended for international travel.
