Frequently Asked Questions

Do you offer home delivery?

We primarily operate as a dine-in restaurant. However, for special events or bulk orders, please contact us directly at +91 98765 43210.

Can I make a reservation?

Yes! Please call us at +91 98765 43210 or use the "Book Table" button. We recommend reservations for groups of 5 or more.

Do you cater for events?

Absolutely! We offer catering services for weddings, corporate events, and celebrations. Contact us for details.

Are you open on holidays?

Yes, we're open 7 days a week, 365 days a year from 7 AM to 10 PM. No holidays!

Is your kitchen pure vegetarian?

Yes, 100%! Our kitchen uses separate equipment to ensure no cross-contamination with non-vegetarian items.

We accept cash, credit cards, debit cards, and digital payment apps like Google Pay, PhonePe, and Paytm.
