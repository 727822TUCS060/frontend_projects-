About Spice Garden

Authentic South Indian Vegetarian Cuisine Since 1995

Our Story

Spice Garden was established in 1995 with a simple mission: to bring authentic South Indian vegetarian cuisine to the people of Trichy. What started as a small family kitchen has grown into a beloved restaurant serving thousands of satisfied customers.

Our founder, with a passion for traditional cooking and a deep respect for culinary heritage, crafted recipes that have been passed down through generations. Every dish served at Spice Garden is a testament to our commitment to authenticity, quality, and taste.

Our Values

  • Authenticity: We use traditional recipes and cooking methods unchanged for decades.
  • Quality: Fresh ingredients sourced daily from trusted suppliers.
  • Vegetarian Purity: 100% vegetarian kitchen with dedicated equipment.
  • Customer Care: Every guest is treated like family.
